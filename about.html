Our story

One oven, a long table, sixteen years

OLIVO started in 2009 with a salvaged wood-fired oven and a stubborn belief that good food shouldn't be complicated.

How it began

We opened in a former baker's on a quiet corner, with forty seats and one oven we rebuilt by hand. The idea was simple: cook what's good, cook it over fire, and let people sit long enough to enjoy it.

Sixteen years on, the oven is the same and the menu still changes with the market. We've added a courtyard, a natural-wine list, and a second-hand pasta machine that refuses to quit — but the premise hasn't moved.

We're a small kitchen on purpose. The person who takes your booking is often the one who plates your pasta.

What we stand for

Four quiet rules

🔥

Cook over fire

The oven and grill give our food its character — char, smoke, and a crust you can't fake.

🥬

Buy from people we know

Growers, fishers, and makers within a day's drive. Names on the menu, not just ingredients.

♻️

Waste less

Whole-animal butchery, veg trim turned into stock, and compost back to the farms we buy from.

🍷

Make it shareable

Plates built for the middle of the table, and a wine list meant to be opened, not studied.
